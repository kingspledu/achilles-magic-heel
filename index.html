Achilles and Hector

This ancient Greek artwork depicts Achilles, the great Greek hero, dragging the body of Hector, the Trojan prince. It represents one of the most dramatic moments of the Trojan War. Achilles seeks revenge for the death of his friend Patroclus. The scene reminds us of the tragedy of war, friendship, and revenge.

CHAPTER 01

Chapter 1. A Very Special Baby

Long, long ago, in a land far away, there lived a king named Peleus.

One day, he had a baby boy.

His name was Achilles.

But Achilles was not an ordinary baby.

His dad was a king. His mom was Thetis, a sea goddess.

"Look at my baby!" said Thetis. "He is so special!"

She wanted her son to be safe and strong.

So, one day, she took him to a magic river called the River Styx.

"This water will keep you safe," she said.

She held Achilles by one heel and dipped him into the water.

Splash!

His body became safe from harm.

But there was one little problem.

The heel she held did not touch the magic water.

It was his one weak spot.

Thetis did not know that this tiny spot would be very important one day.

CHAPTER 02

Chapter 2. The Fastest Boy in Greece

Achilles grew bigger and stronger every day.

He could run very fast.

He could jump high.

He could fight better than anyone else.

"Wow! He is so fast!" people said.

"Look at him go!"

Achilles loved to win.

He trained every day. He wanted to be the best.

Soon, everyone knew his name.

He became the greatest fighter in the Greek army.

But Achilles had something even more special than his strength.

He had a big heart.

And he had a very good friend.

CHAPTER 03

Chapter 3. A Friend Named Patroclus

His friend's name was Patroclus.

They played together.

They trained together.

They laughed together.

When Achilles was happy, Patroclus was happy, too.

When Patroclus had a bad day, Achilles stayed by his side.

"You're my best friend," said Patroclus.

"And you're mine!" said Achilles.

They were like two brothers.

One day, a big war began.

It was called the Trojan War.

The Greeks went to fight the people of Troy.

Achilles and Patroclus went together.

"Stay close to me," said Achilles.

"Don't worry!" said Patroclus. "We can do this!"

But this war would change their lives forever.

CHAPTER 04

Chapter 4. A Fight with a King

The Greek army had a king named Agamemnon.

He was the leader of the army.

But one day, he made Achilles very angry.

He took a prize that belonged to Achilles.

"That is not fair!" said Achilles.

"I will not fight anymore!"

He walked away from the battle.

The other soldiers were worried.

"How can we win without Achilles?" they asked.

But Achilles would not listen.

He was too angry.

He sat in his tent and stayed away from the fight.

Sometimes, even the strongest person can make a bad choice when angry.

CHAPTER 05

Chapter 5. The Sad News

The war went on.

The Greeks were having a hard time.

Patroclus went to see Achilles.

"Please," he said. "Our friends need help."

Achilles looked at him.

"I am still too angry to fight," he said.

"Then let me go," said Patroclus.

He asked to wear Achilles' armor.

Achilles thought for a moment.

"Be careful," he said. "Come back safe."

Patroclus put on the shiny armor.

He went to the battle.

But something terrible happened.

A Trojan hero named Hector saw him.

Hector thought Patroclus was Achilles.

They fought.

And Patroclus did not come back.

He was gone.

CHAPTER 06

Chapter 6. A Broken Heart

When Achilles heard the news, he could not believe it.

"Patroclus? My best friend?"

He felt very sad.

He cried and cried.

"My friend is gone," he said.

He was angry with Hector.

He was angry with himself, too.

"Why did I let him go?" he asked.

"Why did I stay here?"

Achilles knew he could not bring his friend back.

But he wanted to face Hector.

This time, he would go back to the battle.

CHAPTER 07

Chapter 7. The New Armor

Achilles needed new armor.

His old armor was gone.

His mother, Thetis, went to ask Hephaestus for help.

Hephaestus was the god who made things with fire and metal.

He worked in a hot, bright forge.

Clang! Clang! Clang!

He made a new helmet.

He made strong armor.

And he made a beautiful shield.

The shield was amazing!

It showed the sun and the stars.

It showed people dancing and working.

It showed farms, towns, and happy families.

It was like a whole world on one shield!

"Thank you, Mom," said Achilles.

He put on his new armor.

He was ready to fight.

CHAPTER 08

Chapter 8. Achilles and Hector

Outside the walls of Troy, Hector was waiting.

He was a brave prince of Troy.

He was a great fighter, too.

Achilles looked at Hector.

"You took my friend away!" he shouted.

Hector stood tall.

"I will not run away!" he said.

The two heroes began to fight.

Their swords went CLANG!

Their feet moved fast.

The fight was hard.

At last, Achilles won.

Hector fell.

Achilles was still full of anger.

He tied Hector's body to his chariot and dragged it around the city.

It was a sad and cruel end to the fight.

Achilles had won, but his heart was not happy.

His anger had taken control.

CHAPTER 09

Chapter 9. The One Weak Spot

The war was not over.

Achilles was still the strongest fighter.

But every hero has a story.

And every story has an ending.

One day, Paris, a prince of Troy, took his bow.

He saw Achilles.

He pulled back the string.

Whoosh!

The arrow flew through the air.

It hit Achilles in the heel.

His one weak spot!

The magic river had made his body safe, but not that small part.

Achilles fell.

The great hero was gone.

People were very sad.

They remembered his strength.

They remembered his brave heart.

And they remembered his one weak spot.

That is why we still say "Achilles' heel" today.

It means a weak spot that someone has.

CHAPTER 10

Chapter 10. A Hero to Remember

Achilles knew that he had two choices.

He could stay away from the war.

He could live a long and quiet life.

Or he could fight and become a great hero.

But he might die young.

Achilles chose to fight.

He wanted people to remember his name.

And they did!

Even today, people tell stories about Achilles.

They talk about his speed.

They talk about his strength.

They talk about his best friend.

They talk about his anger and his brave heart.

But most of all, they remember that even the greatest hero was not perfect.

And that is what makes his story special.

The End.

AFTER THE STORY

What Can We Learn from Achilles?

Achilles was strong, but he also made mistakes.

His story teaches us some big lessons.

1. Friends are special.

A good friend stays with you in good times and bad times. We should care for our friends and help them when they need us.

2. Anger can cause trouble.

Achilles was so angry that he stopped helping his friends. Before we act, we should stop, take a breath, and think.

3. Nobody is perfect.

Even the strongest hero has a weak spot. We all have things we are good at and things we need to learn.

4. Being brave does not mean being perfect.

A brave person can feel sad, scared, or upset. What matters is learning from mistakes and trying to do better.

AFTER THE STORY

Let's Talk! – A Little Chat with Your Teacher

1. About Friendship

- Why was Patroclus special to Achilles?

- What makes someone a good friend?

- How can you help a friend who feels sad?

2. About Feelings

- Why was Achilles angry with Agamemnon?

- Have you ever been so angry that you wanted to stop playing?

- What can you do when you feel angry?

3. About Being a Hero

- Was Achilles always right?

- Can a person be brave and still make mistakes?

- What makes someone a real hero?

4. Imagine You Are Achilles!

- Would you choose a long, quiet life or a short, exciting life?

- If you had a magic shield, what would you put on it?

- If you could have one superpower, what would it be?
